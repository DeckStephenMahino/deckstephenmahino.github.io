02 / Project Overview

Understanding the Energy Transition

Access to affordable and clean energy remains an important factor in sustainable development. Monitoring progress requires examining multiple indicators across countries and time periods.

This project consolidates energy-related indicators into an interactive Power BI dashboard to explore progress across electricity access, renewable energy, energy efficiency, and financial support.

The analysis aims to make complex information easier to interpret through structured data, meaningful comparisons, and effective visualization.

Project Objectives

  • Evaluate progress across key SDG 7 indicators.
  • Explore changes in global energy performance over time.
  • Examine patterns across countries and indicators.
  • Communicate findings through meaningful visualizations.

Global Monitoring

Bringing multiple indicators together to provide a broader perspective on SDG 7.

04 / Behind the Analysis

From Raw Data to Structured Insights

The project involved preparing, transforming, and organizing datasets before developing the analytical dashboard.

Data Preparation

Cleaning, transforming, and preparing source datasets using Power Query to support consistent and reliable analysis.

Data Modeling

Organizing the analytical dataset using a star-schema structure, connecting the fact table with relevant dimension tables.

Measure Development

Developing DAX measures and calculations to support key performance indicators and analytical reporting.

Data Visualization

Designing visual elements that communicate patterns, comparisons, and performance across energy indicators.
